Privacy policy

Information we collect

We collect the practice and contact details you enter to create a preview or request publishing, such as name, business email, phone number, specialty, city, services and practice description. We also record limited website activity, such as page views and generator steps, to understand and improve the service.

How we use it

We use this information to generate your preview, review a free website publication request, respond to your enquiry, provide requested services, maintain security and improve NiroLife. Business information approved for a free live website—such as the practice name, services, address, hours and public contact details—will be visible to the public and search engines. We do not sell your personal information.

Healthcare information

NiroLife is designed for healthcare business information, not patient care. Do not submit patient records, diagnoses, reports or other sensitive health information.

Storage and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, email and technical service providers used to operate NiroLife. We retain it only as reasonably needed for enquiries, service delivery, security and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete enquiry information, or request review of a published free website, by emailing help@nirolife.com. Some records may be retained where required for security, accounting or legal reasons.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version will remain available on this page.
