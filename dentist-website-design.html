Frequently asked questions

Questions about dentist website design

What should a dentist website include?

It should clearly show the dentist or clinic name, treatments, approved professional information, location, opening hours and working contact or appointment actions.

How much does dentist website design cost?

A NiroLife preview is free. Publishing, a custom domain, additional pages and maintenance are quoted according to the selected package and scope.

Can I show dental treatments on separate pages?

Yes. Additional treatment pages can be created when they add useful, accurate information rather than repeating thin content.

Can patients book appointments through the website?

The website can link to your existing booking system or provide call, WhatsApp and appointment-enquiry actions.

Can I use my dental clinic photographs?

Yes, provided you own the photographs or have permission to publish them and no patient information is exposed without valid permission.

Will my dentist website rank first on Google?

No agency can responsibly guarantee a first position. Technical quality, useful content, local relevance, competition and your broader online presence all affect search performance.
