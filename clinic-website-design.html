Frequently asked questions

Questions about clinic website design

What should a clinic website include?

A clinic website should identify the practice, explain services, introduce relevant practitioners and provide correct hours, location and contact actions.

Can one website cover several specialties?

Yes. A multi-specialty clinic can use organised service categories and additional pages where enough useful content exists.

Can we add more clinic locations later?

Yes. Location pages can be added with unique addresses, hours, contact information and useful local details.

Can clinic staff update the website?

Updates can be submitted to NiroLife under a managed plan. A custom editing arrangement can also be scoped if required.

Can appointment and WhatsApp buttons use different numbers?

Yes. Each approved action can use the most appropriate clinic number or booking destination.

Will NiroLife write medical claims for the clinic?

NiroLife can help organise and edit supplied information, but the clinic must verify professional credentials, clinical claims and regulated content before publication.

Can I create a clinic website preview for free?

Yes. The initial private preview is free and does not require a card or create an obligation to publish.
