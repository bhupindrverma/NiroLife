Frequently asked questions

Questions about healthcare website design

What is healthcare website design?

It is the planning and creation of a website for a healthcare professional or organisation, with clear services, trustworthy information, accessible contact options and careful handling of public claims.

Can I create a preview before paying?

Yes. NiroLife lets you create a private website preview free. Payment is discussed only if you ask to publish or add managed services.

Can the website use my own domain?

Yes. An eligible customer-owned domain can be connected as part of an agreed website launch package.

Will the website work on mobile phones?

Yes. The page structure is designed and tested for common mobile and desktop screen sizes.

Can I add WhatsApp and appointment buttons?

Yes. Call, WhatsApp, directions, email and appointment actions can be connected to the contact flow you approve.

Does a website guarantee patient enquiries?

No. A professional website can improve clarity and accessibility, but rankings, traffic, enquiries, appointments and revenue cannot be guaranteed.

What information is needed to start?

A practice name, profession, city, at least one service and a business contact method are enough for a basic preview. More approved details improve the result.
