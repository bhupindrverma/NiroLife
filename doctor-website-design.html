Frequently asked questions

Questions about doctor website design

What information should a doctor website contain?

It should contain accurate professional details, specialty, services, consultation locations, hours and approved contact options.

Can a doctor have a personal website and a clinic website?

Yes. The right structure depends on whether the site represents one practitioner, a clinic brand or both. Content should avoid unnecessary duplication.

Can qualifications and registration details be shown?

Yes, after the doctor confirms that the information is accurate and appropriate for public display.

Can the website provide medical advice?

The website can publish general, approved educational information, but it should not replace individual medical consultation or collect clinical records through ordinary enquiry forms.

Can patients request an appointment?

Yes. The website can use an enquiry form or link to the doctor’s existing call, WhatsApp or booking process.

Can the website be updated when timings change?

Yes. Updates can be requested individually or handled through an agreed managed-maintenance plan.

Does doctor website design include SEO?

The website includes a sound technical and content structure. Ongoing SEO research, content and local optimisation are separate services when required.
